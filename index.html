Android app · MoneyComing to Google Play

NgernNgokเงินงอก · Money Tracker

Log spending in seconds, scan bank slips and statements, and see where the month is going.

A money tracker for Android, made for people who keep accounts in Thai and English. It is fully usable signed out; signing in with Google turns on sync between devices.

What I built

  • Chat entry: “chicken rice 60” becomes an entry with the amount, category and wallet filled in.
  • Slip scanning through a Supabase Edge Function and Google Gemini. QR codes are read on the phone, and every slip is checked by the user before it is saved.
  • PDF bank-statement import, read on the phone; amounts and dates never leave it.
  • An Android app with Capacitor and on-device SQLite that works offline, plus Google sign-in, sync and a Plus subscription through Google Play Billing.
  • Play Data safety answers derived line by line from the code, a strict Content Security Policy, and a test suite over the money maths.
  • Capacitor · Android
  • Supabase
  • Gemini API
  • English + ไทย
  • Works without an account
  • No ads

Web app · WellbeingLive

AsterismLife Balance Index

A wellbeing self-assessment across eight areas of life, scored against cited Thai and international benchmarks.

Answer a baseline built from validated questionnaires, then check in once a week with rough numbers. Each area gets a grade, a character and a percentile against published population data, and every figure names its source.

Open Asterism (opens in a new tab) Source on GitHub (opens in a new tab)

What I built

  • A scoring engine that maps validated instruments (WHO-5, IPAQ, UCLA-3, CFPB and more) onto eight aspects, with weekly updates and a monthly re-assessment.
  • Percentile benchmarks from Thai national statistics and published surveys, each cited in the interface.
  • A complete English and Thai interface, and rule-based advice that adapts to region, work and relationship status.
  • Local-first by design: no account and no backend, with data kept in the browser under a strict Content Security Policy.
  • Unit tests on the scoring, and Playwright end-to-end flows in CI.
  • Vanilla JS · no build step
  • English + ไทย
  • Open source
  • Data stays on this device
  • No account
